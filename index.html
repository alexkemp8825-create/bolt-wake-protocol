// 01 — HOW IT PLAYS

A top-down minimalist tactical arcade.

In the hangar you build a hex-cell ship around its core. Every cell you place matters: where a module sits decides what it does.

28 cells

Your hull grows cell by cell around the core, up to 28 hex cells.

Three zones

Cells sit in the front, rear or sides of the ship.

Modules by zone

WEAPON → FRONT
DRIVE → REAR
DEFENCE → SIDE

Mirror modules

Fit any cell and reflect enemy fire back.
