// DESCRIPTION

BOLT – Wake Protocol is a top-down minimalist tactical arcade game. In the hangar you build a hex-cell ship around its core. Every cell you place matters: where a module sits decides what it does.

// FEATURES

  • > Build a hex-cell ship around a core: up to 28 cells.
  • > Three zones: front, rear and sides.
  • > Weapon modules go front, drive modules rear, defence modules on the sides.
  • > Mirror modules fit any cell and reflect enemy fire.
